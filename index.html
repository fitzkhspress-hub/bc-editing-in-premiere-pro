YOUR ASSIGNMENT

A 45–60 second news package.

Use your own footage: one interview, a recorded reporter track, and 5–8 relevant shots. Our example is a school food drive. Have your script, headphones, and your teacher’s delivery settings ready.

The guide takes about 8½ minutes to read. Hands-on editing, full playback, and rendering take additional time. No footage or Premiere subscription is included.
